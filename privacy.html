Privacy policy

For the iPhone, iPad, and Android apps, and for the Barry website and Web Workbench.

Effective 25 November 2026

Mobile apps

Barry Barcode Scanner uses your rear camera, with your permission, to find and decode barcodes on your device. The app does not import from your photo library, save camera frames to disk, or send frames or decoded results to the developer or any third party. No account or internet connection is needed to scan. The apps do not use advertising, tracking, or usage analytics.

Scan results stay in memory for the current app session. To delete them sooner, open History, choose Clear, and confirm. Scanner preferences are saved separately on your device so they can be reused. To remove those preferences, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or delete the app from your device.

If you choose a Copy action, the selected result data is written to your system clipboard. Clearing Barry’s History does not clear the clipboard or remove anything you have already pasted or shared. Clipboard handling, including any cross-device syncing, depends on your device and its settings.

You can revoke camera access at any time in your device’s Settings. Barry cannot scan with the camera while permission is off.

Website and Web Workbench

The Barry site does not use advertising, tracking cookies, or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its processing, transfers, and retention practices. We use GitHub Pages to make the site available (our legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The Workbench handles selected images, live camera frames, and scan results in your browser without uploading them. It requests camera access only if you use the camera; you can revoke access in your browser’s site settings. Results kept in Workbench history are session-only and can be cleared in the Results panel.

App stores and external sites you choose to visit have their own privacy policies.

Support email

If you email us, we use your email address, message, and any attachments you send to respond and troubleshoot (our legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Email service providers handle delivery and storage. We keep correspondence only as long as necessary to handle your request, unless a legal obligation or a dispute requires longer retention. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising. Please do not send private barcode data or camera images.
